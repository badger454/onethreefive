# Changelog

Newest first. Each build publishes the top entry as its release notes, and the app shows it under "What's new" when you check for updates.

## Oct 29, 2026 · Nudges, sharing and the dots widget
- Daily nudges: a morning note with your Big Thing (7:00 AM) and an evening check-in (8:00 PM) when something is still unchecked. Change the times or turn them off in Settings → Daily nudges.
- Share to 1-3-5: share text or a link from any app and add it to the bucket or a day. Extra lines go into the task's notes.
- Home-screen widget "1-3-5 dots": looks like the app icon; each dot fills in when its task is checked off, and empty slots are dashed. Tap it to open today.

## Oct 29, 2026 · Lists update instantly
- New tasks appear on today's list right away (they were saved, but the list didn't redraw).
- Edits and deletes show immediately instead of after switching screens.

## Oct 28, 2026 · Typing fix
- Fast typing in an empty line no longer shows "…is full" and loses the text: the line keeps editing the task it just created.

## Oct 27, 2026 · Drop targets follow your finger
- Bucket and Project light up when your finger is over them, not when the middle of the dragged row is.

## Oct 27, 2026 · Cleaner header
- Removed the progress bar and "n of n done" from the header.

## Oct 27, 2026 · Simpler updates
- Check for updates now opens the new version's download in your browser; tap the finished download to install.
- The app no longer asks for permission to install apps, which is what made Play Protect flag 1.0.7.

## Oct 27, 2026 · Updates from Settings
- Settings → Updates → Check for updates: shows what's new and links to the new version.
- Example tasks are now generic.

## Oct 27, 2026 · Typing fix
- Typing into an empty line no longer loses the text after the first letter (Day, Bucket and Projects).

## Oct 26, 2026 · New day header and gestures
- Header shows the month and a Mon–Sun week strip; tap a day to open it, swipe the strip for other weeks.
- Drag the header down to unroll the month calendar; it follows your finger.
- Swiping between days follows your finger and settles on the next day.
- Day rows are one line so a full day fits on screen; tap the text to edit, the arrow to see long text.
- While dragging a task, drop it on Bucket or Project at the bottom of the screen.

## Oct 26, 2026 · First Android version
- The 1-3-5 planner from the HTML prototype as a native app: Day, Bucket, Projects, Settings, repeating tasks, reminders, categories, themes, backup and restore.
